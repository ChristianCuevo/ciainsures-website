Employment Practices Liability Insurance: Is Your Company At Risk?

If your company has employees, your company is at risk for employee claims and lawsuits. Employment practices liability insurance (EPLI) can protect companies from the financial consequences of claims made by current or former employees for claims of discrimination, harassment, wrongful termination, and more.

However, some companies have policies that don’t cover all the costs, don’t cover certain types of claims, or do not include categorizations that correctly identify an employee’s responsibilities.

Do you know if your company has the proper insurance coverage for certain types of complaints? We can help. CIA offers no-cost, line-by-line policy reviews nationwide to help businesses understand what their policies cover and where any vulnerabilities lie.
