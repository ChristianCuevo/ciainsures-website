As a courtesy to our clients, CIA also offers personal insurance, including home insurance, auto insurance, life insurance, home office insurance, umbrella policies, and identity theft insurance. Our brokers will find the right-sized policy that balances your coverage needs and budget, just as we do with our commercial policies.

Home Insurance

CIA will formulate a policy that provides you with the exact coverage you need to protect yourself against home damage, disasters, and vandalism. Liability coverage is also an option under this type of policy. The CIA team works with dozens of providers in all fifty states to find you the proper coverage at a price you can afford.

Auto Insurance

While auto insurance is often a legal requirement, it also provides the peace of mind that comes from knowing that you’re protected, along with your vehicle(s), drivers, and even property. CIA works with a long list of providers nationwide to find the auto insurance policy that meets all of your needs.

Life Insurance

Life insurance offers a valuable safety net for dependents and beneficiaries. A policy can provide financial support and stability to loved ones after the policyholder passes. It’s also a key component in business partnerships. CIA works with insurers across the country to find a life insurance policy that provides peace of mind – without breaking the bank.

Home Office Insurance

With the uptick in people working from home, home office insurance is more popular – and necessary – than ever. This type of insurance protects against virtually any circumstance that could keep the policyholder from working at home, including coverage for business interruption and various home office assets.

Umbrella Insurance

High-worth individuals often need an extra layer of liability coverage above and beyond the limits of many home, auto, or other primary insurance policies. When you need to increase coverage levels, CIA works with dozens of insurers to find the policy that meets your individual needs.

Identity Theft Insurance

Identity theft can result in substantial losses and damages, and it also requires victims to deal with fraud resolution, damaging credit reports, and unlocking frozen accounts. Identity theft insurance not only protects your finances but also offers access to expert teams who can help you resolve all the associated issues. Our brokers work with a long list of insurers in all fifty states, so we can help you find the policy that perfectly protects you and your assets.
