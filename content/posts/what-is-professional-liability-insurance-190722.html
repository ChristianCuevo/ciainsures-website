Professional liability insurance is a type of coverage that protects you against common claims if a customer files a lawsuit over a mistake or bad piece of advice given. This is a risk that can occur at any time in any business, so your company must have this type of coverage. Most professionals are required to carry it. 

What Does Professional Liability Protect Against?

Professional Liability Insurance protects against important risks in your business. This insurance can cover the following types of claims:

  • Breach on contract

  • Negligence 

  • Failure to deliver service in a correct amount of time or to its fullest standard

  • Errors or omissions

Who Needs Professional Liability Coverage?

Basically, any business that provides a service for a fee needs professional liability coverage. Many professional services face risks that need to be covered. These can include but are not limited to:

  • Accountants

  • Home Health Care Companies 

  • Engineers

  • Real Estate Agents

  • Consultants 

  • Alarm Companies 

  • Security Companies
