Alarm Industry Liabilities

Alarm system installers often deal with the maintenance, repair, and occasionally the monitoring of alarm systems. These businesses are the backbone of the alarm industry, and allow for wide-spread use of alarm systems to benefit the general public, as well as key private industries. Those within the industry should understand how important it is to be covered from liabilities in an industry full of them.

It is Central Insurance Agency’s position that alarm companies face serious risks. Alarms that fail to go off during emergencies, as well as alarm monitoring businesses that fail to respond to an emergency in time can face a wide variety of problems. While these incidents may occur spontaneously and with no root cause, it is important to understand how the right insurance plan will be able to help when alarm systems go wrong.

CIA utilizes tailor-made insurance policies that can benefit any business within the alarm industry. Typically, alarm installation companies will face the following liabilities, and as such should consider these policies:

  • Commercial Liability Insurance

  • Professional Liability Insurance

  • Umbrella/Excess Liability

As a general insurance broker, CIA can help with all of your insurance needs and can work with your business to find policies that specifically suit your business. With the right insurance policies, your business will be protected, stronger than ever, and with the ability to ride out the various issues that can plague an alarm installation, monitoring, or repair business.
